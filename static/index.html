    Virtual COMP12111 lab board

    Pick a module that uses the lab board wrapper (Display_Decoder_Board, Traffic_Light_Board, MU0_Board…) and press Start board.

    SV Studio swaps the lab's BoardV3 for a model with the same ports and runs your design live: on the built-in Icarus Verilog (nothing to install), or on Verilator if it's installed, which is about 50x faster. Press keys and flip switches here and watch the displays and lights. Clk_1Hz and Clk_1kHz follow real time, and Clk runs as fast as your PC can simulate it.
